---
name: Reader entry verification
description: Validate discovery and navigation, not just direct player links, when publishing generated editions.
---

Verify the anonymous search → book page → watch path for generated editions, not only a direct player link. Check that unpublished titles still offer requests instead of playback.

**Why:** A generated chapter played correctly through a direct link, while the normal book page incorrectly offered a visual-edition request. Direct player verification had bypassed the reader's actual entry path.

**How to apply:** When publication or availability changes, include a non-sample published edition in the normal navigation check. Keep library saving and progress persistence separate from permission to watch.
